Full description:

Stay focused on what matters — with a private message you write to yourself.
MindWarrior helps you build consistency by bringing you back to your goals and aspirations throughout the day.

How it works:

- Write your “Formula of Firm Resolution” (a private message to yourself): a goal, promise, quote, intention, or all of the above.

- Review it during the day with gentle reminders that help you refocus.

- Turn consistency into a game: earn rewards, keep your streak, unlock levels, and build momentum.

Great for:

- Building habits and self-discipline

- Daily intentions and motivation

- Study sessions and deep work

- Reducing distractions and mindless scrolling

Features:

- Scheduled reminders to review your Formula

- Rewards: diamonds, badges, levels

- Pause + sleep time (no notifications when you don’t want them)

- Markdown support for formatting your Formula

- Difficulty options (from relaxed to strict)

- Works fully offline (no internet required)

Privacy-first by design:

- No account needed

- No ads, no tracking, no analytics

- Your Formula stays on your device

100% free and open source.
